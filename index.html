Early Life and Education

Born and raised in Sandwip, Bangladesh, Sabbir showed a deep connection to music and literature from a young age. He became proficient in keyboard and guitar. He completed his SSC from South Sandwip High School in 2022 and is currently studying at Govt. Hazi A B College.

Music Career

He formed the Oparok Band in 2021 and gained recognition with his album Mritto Bahok. Notable tracks include "Moner Dukkho", "Harano Golpo", and more. His genres include pop, rock, and folk music.

Literature

Sabbir is also an author. His debut book Whispers of the Heart explores themes of love, loss, and self-discovery. His upcoming book The Unseen Dimension will delve into futuristic ideas and deep emotions.
